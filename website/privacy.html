Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Animal Birthday Predictor ("ABP Pro", "we", "us", or "our") collects information to provide stud management, gestation tracking, and certificate generation services for equine and canine breeders. This includes:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password hash, and breeder profile details.
  • Breeding & Animal Records: Mare names, stallion lineage, embryo transfer dates, ultrasound scan records, microchip numbers, and foaling history.
  • Generated Certificates: Entitlement records, certificate IDs, and export logs.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information solely for:

  • Calculating accurate equine foaling due dates and 45-day ultrasound milestones.
  • Rendering official printable PDF certificates.
  • Enforcing certificate quotas and entitlement tracking.
  • Authenticating users and protecting account security via Supabase RLS.

3. Data Security & Storage

All data is transmitted over encrypted SSL/TLS channels and stored securely in cloud database infrastructure with Row-Level Security policies. We do not sell or rent user data to third parties.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Users may request access, modification, or complete deletion of their account and associated animal breeding records by contacting support at support@abp.app.

5. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Office at support@abp.app.
